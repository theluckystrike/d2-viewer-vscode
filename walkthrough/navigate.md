# Pan, zoom and jump to a line

- Drag the diagram to move it. The wheel zooms around the mouse pointer.
- The toolbar has minus, 100% and plus buttons, and Fit to see the whole diagram. With the diagram focused, the keys + and - zoom, 0 fits and 1 shows actual size.
- Click a shape: the editor opens beside the preview at the line where the shape is written, and the shape gets a short outline.
- When the text has an error, the line at the top of the preview names it. Click it to go to that line.
